Privacy Policy

**The short version**
Postgam works entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

**Your photos and videos**
When you add media, you choose exactly which photos and videos to share with the app using Apple's photo picker. Postgam can only see the items you select, not your whole library. Your media is processed on your device to build the preview and the exported video. It is never uploaded to us or to any server.

**Your draft**
So you can pick up where you left off, Postgam keeps a copy of the media you added, your edits, any text and stickers you placed, and any music or voiceover in the app's private storage on your device. This draft is excluded from device backups, is never uploaded anywhere, and is removed if you clear your clips or delete the app.

**Sound**
If a video has sound, it is played in the preview and included in the exported file. You can turn this off or change the level under Audio and Clips. Sound is never sent anywhere.

**Music**
If you add music, you choose an audio file from the Files app. Postgam copies it into the app's private storage and mixes it into the preview and exported video on your device. Only use music you have the right to use.

**Camera and tracking**
Postgam asks for camera access only when you open the finger-frame camera (Edit → Camera). Hand and face tracking use Apple's Vision framework entirely on your device, frame by frame. Nothing is uploaded, no face or hand data is stored, and the live camera picture is never sent anywhere. Only a video you choose to record is saved, as a clip in your draft, and you can turn off camera access at any time in Settings.

**Microphone and voiceover**
Postgam asks for microphone access only when you tap Voiceover or record with the finger-frame camera. It records only while you are recording, saves the take on your device as part of your draft, and mixes it into your video. Recordings are never uploaded, and you can delete a take or turn off access at any time in Settings.

**Saving and sharing**
Postgam only asks permission to add to your Photos library, and only when you tap Save Video. It cannot read or change anything already in your library. If you tap Share, the video is handed to the app or service you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies from that point.

**Temporary files**
Exported videos are stored briefly in the app's temporary storage on your device. iOS clears this automatically, and deleting the app removes everything.

**Purchases**
Postgam Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or any other personal information. The app only learns whether Pro has been unlocked, and stores that on your device so it can load quickly.

**Stored on your device**
Besides your draft, Postgam saves two small flags on your device: whether Postgam Pro is unlocked, and whether you have seen the intro. They never leave your device. Postgam does not use cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

**The watermark**
Free exports include a small "Postgam" tag, drawn onto the exported video (or any exported image) on your device. It is plain visible text. No information about you, your media or your device is hidden in it, and it doesn't track anything. It does not appear in the live preview. Postgam Pro removes it from new exports.

**What we don't do**
We don't collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data. We don't use third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

**Children**
Postgam doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Changes**
If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here in the app, with a new date above.
