Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version
POST works entirely on your device. We don’t collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your photos and videos
When you add media, you choose exactly which photos and videos to share with the app using Apple’s photo picker. POST can only see the items you select, not your whole library. Your media is processed on your device to build the preview and the exported video. It is never uploaded to us or to any server.

Sound
If a video has sound, it is played in the preview and included in the exported file. You can turn this off under Media → Order → Play video sound. Audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Saving and sharing
POST only asks permission to add to your Photos library, and only when you tap Save Video. It cannot read or change anything already in your library. If you tap Share, the video is handed to the app or service you pick, and that app’s own privacy policy applies from that point.

Temporary files
Selected videos and exported videos are stored briefly in the app’s temporary storage on your device while you work. iOS clears this automatically, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases
POST Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or any other personal information. The app only learns whether Pro has been unlocked, and stores that on your device so it can load quickly.

Stored on your device (new)
POST saves two small flags on your device: whether POST Pro is unlocked, and whether you have seen the intro. They never leave your device.

The watermark (new)
Free exports include a small “Made with POST” tag, drawn onto the video on your device. It is plain visible text. No information about you, your media or your device is hidden in it, and it doesn’t track anything. POST Pro removes it.

What we don’t do
We don’t collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data. We don’t use third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics services.

Children
POST doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here in the app, with a new date above.

Contact
Questions about privacy? Email plugcalapp@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and message only so we can reply, and we don’t share them.
